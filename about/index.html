A little bit about myself

I spent my undergraduate and graduate studies at UCLA, where I obtained my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering. In my time there, I was a part of (and eventually managed) Bruin Underwater Robotics (BUR), a student-led project under ASME at UCLA. I continued my pursuit of engineering by obtaining my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering, with a focus in Systems and Controls.

The field of controls fascinates me -- I find there is a deep beauty in the interconnection between complex math and the "personality" inherent to different robots, machines, and devices.

Outside of engineering, I have a deep love for music and gaming. I can be found playing the viola, electric bass, and zhonghu in my free time, even playing for the Game Music Ensemble at UCLA.
