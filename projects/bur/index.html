Bruin Underwater Robotics

Bruin Underwater Robotics (BUR) is a student-led engineering project under ASME at UCLA. I was invlolved from 2021-2025, entering as a general member under the electronics team, working up as subteam lead for the structures team, and eventually running and managing the club as a project lead. The goal of the project was to design and manufacture submersible robots for the MATE ROV and Robosub competitions. These robots were meant to perform a variety of tasks related to practical applications for underwater vehicles.

2024-2025: Project Lead

In my final year with BUR, I stepped into the role of a Project Lead for the club.

2023-2024: Chassis Lead

This year marked the project's debut to the Robosub AUV competition. As it was necessary that the robot operate autonomously, my role as Chassis Lead was to design a structural frame that could accommodate features previously relegated to remain on the surface (compressed air and computer processing) . In addition, my role included directing a team of six engineers and helping them develop skills in CAD-ing and manufacturing. Our robot, Ambition, ranked 21st against 41 participating teams from across the globe.

2022-2023: DiveOps Lead

The DiveOps team was the division of the project responsible for designing solutions for compeition-related tasks. In this position, I was to guide a small team of general members through the production of a secondary robot. This robot was meant to submerge, resurface, and to send a message to a main computer. We achieved this by integrating a servo-driven buoyancy engine into a neutrally buoyant vehicle, which could traverse the water column by intaking and expelling water from the environment.

2021-2022: General Member

This season marked not only my first year on the project, but the first year BUR operated in-person. As a member of the Onboard Electronics Systems, my responsibilities were two-part: 1) to research necessary eletronics components and design the components-level wiring diagram and 2) to manufacture and test the electronics bay for the onboard electronic components. We entered our vehicle to the MATE ROV compeition, where we placed 2nd at our regional competition and qualified for the World Finals.
